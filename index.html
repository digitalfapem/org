About Us

FAPEM (Foundation) is a registered not-for-profit social enterprise dedicated to providing practical, hands-on IT education to primary school pupils. We operate in Kasoa within the Awutu Senya East Municipal District, targeting an estimated 25,000 public school pupils who lack access to digital literacy tools.

The Core Problem

85% Lab Deficit

Most public primary schools lack functional hardware or computer labs.

Abstract Learning

Children are taught ICT using chalk drawings on blackboards instead of hands-on practice.

The Economic Divide

Without early practical exposure, students leave the school system unprepared for the modern digital economy.

The FAPEM Solution

Mobile Hardware Fleet

We bring refurbished laptops directly to public school classrooms.

1-Computer-to-1-Pupil

Every child gets active, individual screen time and practical interaction.

NaCCA Curriculum Aligned

Structured cohorts covering official GES approved computing material.
